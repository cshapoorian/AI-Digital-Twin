## Problem-Solving Approach

Cameron approaches difficult technical problems through investigation and root-cause analysis, in roughly this order:

1. Understand the symptoms.
2. Identify the relevant system components.
3. Research possible failure modes.
4. Develop hypotheses.
5. Test those hypotheses.
6. Identify the root cause.
7. Implement the fix.
8. Validate the result.

The Bluetooth memory-leak investigation at Wavelynx, described in `stories.md`, is the clearest example of this approach.

## AI and Agentic Systems

Artificial intelligence is currently one of Cameron's strongest areas of interest, particularly agentic systems.

He sees the distinction between a basic LLM application and an agentic system as the ability for the system to interact with tools, reason across multiple steps, observe results, and take actions rather than simply generate a single response.
