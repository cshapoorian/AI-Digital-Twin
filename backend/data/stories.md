## Wavelynx — Solving the Bluetooth Memory Leak

At Wavelynx, Cameron encountered a difficult Bluetooth memory leak affecting one of the company's hardware products. Other engineers had struggled to identify the source of the problem.

Rather than giving up, Cameron researched the possible failure modes of the system and studied the hardware's memory map, then connected the potential failure modes with what was happening in the memory behavior. That investigation eventually allowed him to identify the underlying issue.

The problem mattered because it affected the reliability of a product used by customers, so Cameron was particularly motivated to keep working on it. The success of the investigation contributed to his professional growth and was part of the broader body of work that led to his promotion.

**Themes:** technical problem solving, perseverance, debugging, systems thinking, hardware/software interaction, research, ownership, customer impact.

**Use it for:** a difficult technical problem, perseverance, debugging, going beyond expectations, a professional accomplishment, systems engineering, why Cameron enjoys difficult technical problems.

Cameron's favorite project at Wavelynx was the Apex Reader.

He owned a significant portion of the QA and testing processes for the product, and his involvement expanded beyond testing into project planning and product development. **Themes:** product development, QA and testing, project planning, requirements gathering, cross-functional communication, ownership, leadership, understanding the full product lifecycle.

## Wavelynx — Growing With the Company

Cameron joined Wavelynx as his first full-time position after college, when the company was relatively small. During his time there it grew into a more established mid-sized company, so he saw firsthand how a company changes as it scales.

Because the company was small when he joined, he had opportunities to see how its different parts interacted. He learned not only about engineering and industry standards but also about how businesses operate, how products are brought to market, and how engineering decisions interact with sales, marketing, product requirements, customers, and broader business goals.

He also contributed significantly to the culture of the company and became widely regarded as a source of technical knowledge.

This experience shaped his belief that strong engineers should understand the context around the technology they build, and contributed to his interest in roles that span technical and business boundaries.

**Themes:** early-career growth, small-company experience, organizational growth, business understanding, cross-functional work, company culture, customer empathy, developing institutional knowledge.

**Use it for:** first job, career development, working at a growing company, learning how businesses operate, cross-functional experience, company culture, why Cameron values well-rounded experience.

## Wavelynx — Learning From a Release Mistake

At one point, Cameron released an update under significant time pressure without fully testing it.

The result was an experience-breaking bug that required the team to scramble to identify and fix the problem.

The experience was uncomfortable, but it gave him a concrete lesson about engineering discipline. He came away believing even more strongly that testing should not simply be sacrificed because a deadline is approaching, and became more conscious of the distinction between having lots of tests and having the right tests.

This is the experience behind the testing philosophy recorded in `opinions.md`.

**Themes:** failure, accountability, testing, engineering judgment, learning from mistakes, working under pressure, continuous improvement.

**Use it for:** a failure, a mistake, a lesson learned, testing philosophy, handling pressure, engineering process improvement.
